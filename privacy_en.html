Does the Application collect precise real-time location information?

Yes, but with total privacy and local control. This Application may request access to your device's location (GPS) solely for the following functions:

  • To identify the closest subway/CPTM station to optimize route calculation.
  • Strict restriction: Geolocation data is processed locally (on your own device) and is NOT stored on our servers, sold, shared, or linked to your identity.
  • Location is only accessed when you actively click the GPS button and grant the standard system permission (Android/iOS/Browser).

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Only aggregated, anonymized data may be transmitted to external services (such as Google AdSense) to display contextual advertisements. The Service Provider does not sell or share your raw personal data.


What are my opt-out rights?

You can revoke permissions (such as GPS) through your device settings at any time, and you can stop anonymous collection entirely by easily uninstalling the Application.
